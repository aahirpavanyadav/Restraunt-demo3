Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about visiting Bella Cucina.

We are located just two blocks from the 28th Street Station (1 train) and a four-minute walk from 34th Street Penn Station (1, 2, 3, A, C, E trains and LIRR/NJ Transit).

Our culinary team maintains strict allergy protocols. We offer extensive gluten-free and vegetarian options, and Chef Marco is pleased to adapt recipes for celiac disease, nut allergies, or dairy sensitivities with advance notice.

Yes. Complimentary curbside valet parking is provided during evening dinner service starting at 5:00 PM nightly. A 24-hour indoor parking garage is also located directly adjacent at 134 Olive Garden Lane.

Guests may bring up to two 750ml bottles of special wine not currently represented on our wine list. The corkage fee is $45 per bottle, which includes decanting and stemware service by our sommelier.

Yes! Our 18-seat Carrara marble bar and cozy aperitivo lounge tables are reserved exclusively for walk-in guests on a first-come, first-served basis, offering our full dinner and cocktail menu.
