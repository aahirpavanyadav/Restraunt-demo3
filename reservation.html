Guest Guidelines

Dining Policies & Etiquette

To ensure an exquisite experience for all patrons, please review our policies.

⏱️

Cancellation Policy

We hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past scheduled booking time. For cancellations or party adjustments, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a \$25 per guest fee.

👔

Dress Code

We invite guests to embrace upscale smart casual attire. We kindly request gentleman refrain from athletic shorts, flip-flops, and gym wear in the main dining rooms.

👶

Children Welcome

Families are cherished at Bella Cucina. We gladly provide high chairs and our culinary brigade happily prepares custom fresh pasta portions suited for young gourmets.

Private Dining & Milestone Celebrations

Planning a private corporate dinner, rehearsal dinner, or intimate wine tasting? Our private 'Sala Firenze' accommodates up to 55 guests with bespoke menus and dedicated sommelier service.
