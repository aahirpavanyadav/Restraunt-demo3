Meet Master Chef Marco Rossi

A lifelong devotion to Italian culinary heritage, unyielding technique, and genuine warmth.

👨‍🍳

Chef Marco Rossi

Executive Chef & Founder

Born in Florence, trained across Modena, Milan, and Paris. Over 28 years dedicated to Italian gastronomy.

Master of the Craft

Culinary Passion Passed Through Generations

Chef Marco Rossi was raised in a farmhouse just outside Florence where mornings began with gathering fresh eggs, foraging wild herbs in the Tuscan hills, and rolling pasta beside his grandmother, Nonna Teresa.

After graduating from the prestigious Culinary Institute of Florence and working in three-star Michelin establishments across Italy and France, Marco arrived in New York with a singular ambition: to create an unpretentious, world-class sanctuary where diners taste the true soul of Italy without compromise.

Chef Marco's Signature Creations:

  • ✻ Tagliolini al Tartufo — 30-egg yolk pasta, mountain butter, shaved black truffles
  • ✻ Bistecca alla Fiorentina — 45-day dry aged porterhouse seared over oak coals
  • ✻ Ravioli di Zucca con Salvia — Roasted squash, amaretto crumble, browned sage
  • ✻ Risotto all'Amarone — Carnaroli rice, Valpolicella Amarone, roasted bone marrow

— Chef Marco Rossi, Executive Chef & Proprietor

Matteo Bellini

Chef de Cuisine

A Bologna native who leads our pasta laboratory. Matteo oversees every millimeter of pasta dough kneaded, extruded, and folded every morning.

Giulia Romano

Executive Pastry Chef
