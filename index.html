• Sourcing & Philosophy •

The Rhythm of Soil & Embers

Every morning, crates arrive from coastal farms, biodynamic vineyards, and regenerative fisheries along the Pacific.

Regenerative Growers

We partner with 48 certified heirloom family farms practicing no-till agriculture, preserving nutrient density and pure terroir flavor.

Live Hearth Cooking

No gas ranges. Our kitchen harnesses radiant heat from fallen olive wood and white oak, searing whole fish and caramelizing root vegetables.

Living Natural Cellar

Curated low-intervention, unfiltered, and orange wines from small European and Californian vintners working without synthetic additives.
