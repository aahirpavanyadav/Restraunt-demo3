Our Story & Heritage

A culinary journey rooted in ancestral Tuscan traditions and elevated with modern gastronomic passion.

A Passion Born In Tuscany

From Florence to the Heart of Manhattan

Founded in 2010 by Master Chef Marco Rossi, Bella Cucina was born out of a desire to preserve the sacred authenticity of Italian cooking while creating a warm, elegant haven for discerning New York food lovers.

Growing up in the hills of Florence, Chef Marco learned to make pasta before he could ride a bicycle. His nonna taught him that every meal is an expression of affection, requiring patience, authentic provenance, and deep respect for every ingredient that touches the flame.

Today, Bella Cucina stands as a testament to that legacy. Every ribbon of pasta is kneaded fresh daily in our glass kitchen, every sauce simmers slowly for hours, and our sommelier curates the finest vintages from boutique estates across Piedmont, Tuscany, and Sicily.

Growth & Milestones

Our Culinary Journey Through Time

From our humble opening to becoming an iconic Manhattan dining staple.

2010

The Doors Open in Manhattan

Chef Marco Rossi opens Bella Cucina with a cozy 40-seat dining room and a 4-person pasta kitchen on Olive Garden Lane.

2013

First Major Accolade

Awarded "Best Authentic Italian Restaurant" by the NYC Culinary Review and listed among New York’s top dining discoveries.

2016

Cellar & Private Dining Expansion

Expanded into the adjoining historical brownstone, building our custom climate-controlled wine cellar and the 'Sala Firenze' private dining suite.

2020

Gourmet Pantry & Online Offerings

Launched our signature meal kits, digital reservations, and artisanal Italian pantry line to bring Bella Cucina into patrons' homes.

2024

Architectural Renovation & Michelin Honors

Completed a lavish multi-month renovation featuring custom terrazzo floors, acoustic design, and earned renewed Michelin Guide Selection status.
