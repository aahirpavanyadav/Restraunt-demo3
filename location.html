Transportation

Getting to Bella Cucina

Conveniently accessible by subway, car, and city transit.

🚇

By Subway

  • 1 Train: Exit at 28th Street Station (2-minute walk west).
  • A, C, E, 1, 2, 3 Trains: 34th Street Penn Station (4-minute walk south).
  • N, Q, R, W Trains: 28th St & Broadway (6-minute walk west).
🚗

By Car & Taxi

  • From West Side: Take West Side Highway (Route 9A) to West 28th Street.
  • From East Side: Take FDR Drive exit at 34th Street and cross westbound.
  • Taxis & Rideshare: Set destination to "128 Olive Garden Lane".
🚌

By Bus

  • M7 Bus: Drops directly at 7th Avenue & West 28th Street.
  • M20 Bus: Stops at 7th Avenue & West 27th Street.
  • M34-SBS Crosstown: 34th Street bus stop (6-minute walk).
Valet & Garages

Seamless Arrival & Valet Parking

We strive to make your arrival as relaxing as your meal. Complimentary curbside valet service is offered directly outside our front entrance for all evening dinner guests beginning at 5:00 PM nightly.

For patrons arriving for lunch or preferring self-parking, our verified partner garage—Icon Parking Garage—is located directly adjacent at 134 Olive Garden Lane. Bring your parking ticket to our host stand for 2 hours of validated parking discount.
